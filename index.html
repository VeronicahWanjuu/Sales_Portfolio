My Story

From University Entrepreneur to Revenue Driver

My journey in sales began during university, where I started selling apartments to fellow students. While the extra income was nice, what truly captivated me was the moment when a student's face lit up after finding exactly what they needed. That's when I realized sales isn't about pushing products—it's about understanding problems and delivering solutions that genuinely improve people's lives.

This insight has driven my entire career approach. I don't just make calls or send emails. I study businesses, understand their challenges, and position solutions that create real value.

Over the past three years, I've worked remotely with companies across the world as both an employee and contractor. This diverse experience has given me a unique view of different markets, business models, and cultural differences that make outreach more effective.
